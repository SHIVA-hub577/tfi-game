How to Play TFI Auction

👥

1. Setup & Equal Budgets

2, 3, or 4 players play on one device. Everyone starts with the same budget of coins. Choose Single Topic mode for one hero's film list, or Multi Topic mode to combine two or more heroes into one big auction pool.

🔨

2. Opening the Auction

Movies are auctioned one by one. The rotating opener places the opening bid at 1 coin. Before opening, each player may pass on a movie — every player must pass before it is discarded as unsold.

📈

3. Bid, Raise, or Drop

Turn passes clockwise. On your turn you can Raise or Drop. Minimum raise is 1 coin up to 20, and 2 coins above 20. When a player drops, the turn always moves to the next player still bidding — the movie is only sold once a single bidder remains.

🛡️

4. The Reserve Rule

You can never get stuck without coins! You must reserve at least 1 coin for each other movie you still need to win: Max Bid = Coins Left − (Movies Needed − 1). The screen automatically displays your max bid.

🎬

5. Winning & Game End

When all other players drop, the last standing bidder wins the movie, pays the bid, and the movie joins their collection. Once you reach your limit, you sit out. The game ends when everyone has won their quota!
